The problem

Public bids are scattered everywhere.

Government, school district, park district, transit and municipal projects are posted across dozens of portals and plan rooms. Each one has its own login, its own format and its own schedule.

Checking them all takes hours every week, so most estimators check a few and hope. Good jobs get missed, and not because the contractor was not qualified. Nobody saw the notice in time. A missed prebid meeting can end a bid before the documents are even opened.

How it works

Three steps. You only do the last one.

  1. 1

    We scan public bid sources

    Every week we go through public bid sources across the region.

  2. 2

    We filter for your trade

    We keep the roofing bids in your area and drop expired or irrelevant ones.

  3. 3

    You get one email

    The open bids with key dates and links, plus a short list of bids closing soon.

Where do the bids come from?

From public postings by government, school district, park district, transit and municipal owners, and from the portals and plan rooms that publish them. Every bid links back to its source.

How often does it arrive?

Once a week, by email.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. The plan is monthly and there is no contract.

What areas are covered?

What if my trade is not listed?

Yes. Bid details are summarized from public postings. Confirm dates, addenda and requirements on the source page before you bid.
