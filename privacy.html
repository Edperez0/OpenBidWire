Privacy

Last updated October 29, 2026

OpenBidWire is a weekly email of open public bids for contractors. This page explains what information we collect and what we do with it. We keep it short on purpose.

What we collect

  • Free sample form. The name, company, email, trade and message you type into the form.
  • Email you send us. Your address and whatever you write.
  • Subscription details. When you subscribe, Stripe collects your payment information and email. We receive your name, email and subscription status. We never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

  • To send you the free sample and reply to your messages.
  • To send you the weekly issue if you subscribe, and to manage your subscription.
  • To decide which trades and regions to add next.

We do not sell your information. We do not share it for advertising.

Who handles it for us

  • Formspree receives the free sample form and passes it to us.
  • Stripe processes payments and subscriptions.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Stripe may set its own cookies on its checkout pages.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your details while you are a subscriber or in touch with us. To have them deleted, email us and we will remove them.

Weekly emails

Every issue has an unsubscribe link. You can also reply to any issue and ask to be removed.

Changes

If we change this page, we will update the date at the top.
